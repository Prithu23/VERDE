The Problem — Disaster Response is Blind, Slow & Costly

  • 🚨 Rescue teams enter collapse zones with zero information on gas, flooding, or survivors
  • ⏱ Manual recon wastes the first 72 hours — the golden survival window
  • ☠️ NDRF & fire personnel face explosive gas and toxic air with no prior warning
  • 💰 Commercial disaster robots cost ₹2 Lakh – ₹20 Lakhs — unaffordable for NGOs
  • 📉 Less than 4% of Indian municipalities have robotic disaster response
  • 🇮🇳 India records ~8,000+ disaster fatalities/year — ~65% preventable with early detection

Solution — AEGIS (Autonomous Emergency Guidance & Intelligence System)

  • 🧠 Triple AI Vision: YOLOv8 (people) + rubble.pt + oil.pt — live at 20+ fps
  • 🌡️ 8-Sensor Fusion: Temp · Humidity · Pressure · GPS · MPU-6050 · Water · MQ2 · MQ4 · MQ135
  • ⚡ Critical Alert: MQ2 ≥ 40% triggers instant EVACUATE banner on dashboard
  • 🗺️ Live GPS Map: Every detection event is geotagged and logged automatically
  • 📊 React Dashboard: Camera feed, gas charts, gyro tilt, alert feed — all live
  • 📱 SMS Dispatch: Alerts sent to registered responders on threshold breach

Pipeline: ESP32 → sensor_reader.py → Fusion Engine → EHS Calculator → Dashboard → SMS
